# Surface brief: landing page (v4, replaces the lantern and leaf worlds)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: Sinhala-speaking beginners on phones from Facebook ads. Job: click the WhatsApp interest group CTA for a PAID course. Constraints: content from landing-page.json, ids and analytics untouched, no fabricated proof, paid status clear, reduced motion respected.

User feedback: the lantern world felt cheap and childish; the leaf manuscript felt like "the worst" (texture, typography and colour all disliked). The user chose "Bright, modern, clean" and asked for typography fixes. This is the category standard played straight (the standing exit), executed at the craft level of top online-academy and SaaS landing pages.

Build path: code-led.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm, confident course page: white space, one strong blue, rounded cards and clear Sinhala type. The page shows the learner's route (idea to launch) at once and earns trust through clarity and honesty about the paid status, not decoration. Refuses texture, themed motifs, candy colour fields and dark-mode drama.

OWN-WORLD: Near-white page, soft blue-grey bands, one saturated blue (oklch 50% 0.21 262) used for primary buttons, key cards and two full bands (mission, final). A soft amber pill and amber note carry the "paid course" disclosure. Figtree with Noto Sans Sinhala at normal width, weights 400 to 750, no condensed or poster type. 12 to 20px radii, one soft layered shadow, Lucide line icons at one stroke weight in blue-tint circles. A night-navy footer.

STORY: Visitor sees a clear headline and a five-step learning route beside it, understands it is a paid practical course, scans learning topics and what they could build, tries the honest calculator, trusts the 10 year freelancer, taps the WhatsApp group knowing nothing is free and nothing is guaranteed.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: white nav; left column amber pill "Upcoming Paid Practical Training", large Sinhala headline, subline, blue CTA, short note, amber disclosure; right column a white roadmap card listing the five steps with line icons on a connecting line. Phone: same order stacked, CTA reachable in the first screen.

FORM: Standard exit (clean professional), played straight.

MOTION: roadmap steps ease in on load; blocks below the fold fade up once; a thin blue progress line on the nav; card hover lift; rails on phones; all disabled under reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
